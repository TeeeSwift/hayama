# Junpei's Father

## Overview

Junpei's father is a long-time resident of [[Atlas/Hayama|Hayama]] with deep ties to the sea and the town's coastal culture. He has spent much of his life around fishing, diving, and maritime work, and is quietly respected among locals.

He represents an older generation that is more accustomed to Hayama's strange rhythms, even if he does not openly explain them.

## Personality

- Calm and practical
- Speaks in short, grounded observations
- Rarely surprised by unusual events
- Protective of Junpei, but encourages independence

## Relationship To Junpei

- Taught Junpei how to swim and dive from a young age
- Encouraged her to become a diving instructor
- Occasionally gives cryptic advice about "respecting the sea"

## Possible Secrets / Hooks

- Knows more about the changes in the water than he admits
- Has witnessed signs of [[Lore/The Beckoning Tide|The Beckoning Tide]] before
- May have quiet connections to [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]] or other long-standing local figures

## First Session Use

- Can appear briefly before Junpei leaves to teach her diving class
- Offers a small warning or strange comment about the water that day
- Serves as a grounding emotional anchor before the strange events begin
