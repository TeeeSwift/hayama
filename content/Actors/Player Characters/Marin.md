# Marin

## Elevator Pitch

A local high school diver who unknowingly carries displaced divinity from an
improper revival, causing the ocean itself to respond to her differently.

---

## Surface Identity

- High school student
- Assists her father with diving instruction on weekends
- Deeply respectful of the ocean

She was raised to approach the sea with:

- discipline
- caution
- reverence

---

## Social Context

Marin has a friend who teaches diving at Zushi Beach.

That environment is:

- trendy
- aesthetic
- social
- performative

Marin feels:

- jealousy toward that freedom
- discomfort with how casually the ocean is treated

This creates an internal tension:

> Reverence vs Performance

---

## Hidden Nature

Marin is one of [[Actors/The Drifted|The Drifted]].

Her condition originates from [[Actors/Zenji|Zenji]], an ancestor who was
recently revived by [[Actors/Teru|Teru]].

Because Zenji exists outside his intended lifecycle, his divinity did not
settle correctly.

Instead, it manifested in Marin.

She does not know:

- about Zenji
- about the divinity
- that anything supernatural is happening

---

## First Incident: The Ocean

During a diving class, Marin assists her father while Ryuta participates as a
student.

While diving, she descends slightly deeper than the others.

### Sensory Shift

- sound dulls
- pressure feels incorrect
- the water feels familiar

### Core Phenomenon

The ocean stops resisting her.

- she sinks too easily
- movement becomes effortless
- she feels like she belongs in the depth

### Critical Moment

She realizes:

> she is not trying to go back up

She is not trapped.

She is misaligned.

### Resolution

Her father pulls her back to the surface.

The sensation breaks immediately.

The surface feels:

- loud
- heavy
- wrong

---

## Interpretation

This is not the ocean attacking her.

This is:

> the ocean recognizing misplaced divinity

---

## Aftermath

Marin experiences:

- subtle fear of depth
- confusion about her own instincts
- a growing disconnect from normal ocean experience

---

## Thematic Role

Marin represents:

- Misplaced inheritance
- Identity shaped by forces she does not understand
- The boundary between human systems and natural systems
- What happens when divinity settles incorrectly

---

## Campaign Role

- Early: Unexplained and deeply personal anomaly
- Mid: Recognition that her experience is not isolated
- Late: Confrontation with the origin and consequences of her condition

Her existence reflects instability within the broader system governing
Hayama.
