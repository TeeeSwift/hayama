## Barrier Destabilization Effects

- Reclaiming events resume in fragmented, unnatural ways.
- Suppressed divinity leaks back out across Hayama.
- The [[Lore/The Barrier|Relief Chamber]] overflows or backflows.
- [[Lore/The Artifact|The Artifact]] wakes up and creates fae-like mountain
  entities.
- [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]] become more visible as containment work
  escalates.
