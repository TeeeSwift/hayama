# Scallops

## Role

Scallops is an Anchored concept for watchfulness, sudden escape, and delicate
life protected by a hinged shell.

Use this concept for an Anchored who seems decorative or passive until they move
all at once.

## Natural Model

Scallops are bivalves with fan-shaped shells. Unlike many bivalves, scallops can
swim short distances by rapidly clapping their shells and jetting water.

Many scallops also have rows of simple eyes along the mantle edge, giving them a
wide field of environmental awareness.

This makes Scallops a figure of:

- hidden watchfulness
- beauty with sudden mobility
- retreat by rhythm and pressure
- many small eyes instead of one commanding gaze
- protection that opens and closes

## Campaign Use

Scallops should represent the observer everyone underestimates.

They may notice small changes in light, water, attention, and body language.
They may withdraw abruptly when threatened, leaving only a closed shell of
politeness behind.

Use Scallops when the campaign needs:

- a decorative NPC who saw everything
- a sudden retreat from a social scene
- a character whose awareness comes from many small perceptions

## Table Motifs

- fan shapes
- repeated opening and closing gestures
- many small mirrors or lenses
- someone watching exits through reflections
- a sudden clap, snap, or movement when danger comes too close
