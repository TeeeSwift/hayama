# Vincent

## Elevator Pitch

A wealthy local ranger from an old Hayama family, unknowingly tied to a long
lineage of Drifted descendants and a quiet, persistent connection that has
followed his bloodline for generations.

---

## Surface Identity

- Background: Local, affluent family
- Profession: Ranger
- Disposition: Grounded, capable, accustomed to stability

Vincent has grown up with a sense of familiarity and ownership over Hayama.
The town is not strange to him—it is home.

---

## Hidden Significance

Vincent is a descendant of [[Actors/The Drifted|The Drifted]] through his
mother’s lineage, part of a bloodline that traces back to the earliest
generations of Hayama.

This lineage carries a subtle but persistent connection to divine patterns,
though Vincent is not consciously aware of it.

---

## Family

- Father: Successful businessman with modern influence
- Mother: From an old Hayama family with deep historical roots

His family represents a convergence of:
- modern power
- inherited legacy

---

## The Housemaid

Vincent’s household has long employed a housemaid who has served the family
across generations.

She appears entirely normal:
- composed
- professional
- unremarkable in demeanor

Vincent is aware of a rumor:

- that her father was responsible for burning the royal villa in town

This rumor has never been confirmed, but it lingers as a quiet mark against her
otherwise impeccable presence.

---

## True Nature (Hidden)

The housemaid is one of [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]].

She embodies one half of a phenomenon often described as the “miracle” of deep
sea creatures finding one another in the dark.

Long ago, she was in love with one of Vincent’s ancestors. As that ancestor
began to fade into mortality, she made a promise:

> that she would find them again, somehow, through their descendants.

She has remained near this bloodline ever since.

---

## Relationship to Vincent

Vincent is not the same person as his ancestor, but he carries a similar
pattern.

The housemaid does not treat him as a replacement.

Instead:
- she observes
- she remains present
- she waits

Her connection to him is:
- patient
- unresolved
- shaped more by time than by expectation

---

## Thematic Role

Vincent represents:

- Continuity across generations
- Inheritance without understanding
- The persistence of connection over time

He contrasts with figures like [[Ayaka]], who embody transformation and change.

---

## Campaign Role

- Early: Grounded perspective within a destabilizing town
- Mid: Discovery of his family’s deeper connection to Hayama
- Late: Confrontation with a relationship that has outlived its origin

His choices may determine whether inherited patterns are preserved, broken, or
redefined.
