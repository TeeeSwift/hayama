# Sound Of Waves

## Role

Sound Of Waves is an Anchored concept for rhythm, masking, memory, and the way
repetition can soothe or obscure.

Use this concept for an Anchored who works through sound, timing, and what
people stop hearing because it is always present.

## Natural Model

Wave sound is produced by water breaking, rushing, dragging sediment, trapping
air, and retreating. It can be calming, loud, directional, masking, and
hypnotically repetitive.

People near the shore often stop consciously noticing it until it changes.

This makes Sound Of Waves a figure of:

- constant background presence
- repetition that becomes memory
- masking important sounds
- warning through change in rhythm
- comfort that can hide danger

## Campaign Use

Sound Of Waves should represent unnoticed systems that only become meaningful
when they fall out of rhythm.

They may notice when the Barrier loses tempo, when a recording has impossible
background audio, or when a witness omits the one sound everyone should have
heard.

Use Sound Of Waves when the campaign needs:

- audio clues
- missing sounds
- a character who hears systemic change before others see it

## Table Motifs

- recordings with wrong surf in the background
- conversations covered by passing waves
- sudden silence
- repeated phrases that feel like tide
- a person who asks everyone to stop talking and listen
