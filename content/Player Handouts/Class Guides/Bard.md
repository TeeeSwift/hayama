# Bard Class Guide

This guide is for reading your sheet during play. Your sheet is the source of truth.

Spell list: [[Player Handouts/Spell Lists/Bard Level 1 Spells|Bard Level 1 Spells]]

## As A Bard, You Start With These Things At Level 1

At level 1, you are a social, magical, skillful support character. You start with:

- **Hit Points:** 8 + your Constitution modifier.
- **Hit Dice:** one d8.
- **Armor training:** Light armor.
- **Weapon proficiency:** Simple weapons.
- **Tools:** Three musical instruments of your choice.
- **Saving throws:** Dexterity and Charisma.
- **Skills:** Any three skills of your choice.
- **Spellcasting:** Two cantrips, four prepared level 1 bard spells, and two level 1 spell slots.
- **Spellcasting ability:** Charisma.
- **Bardic Inspiration:** Use a Bonus Action to give another creature an inspiration die that can help a d20 test.

Starting equipment depends on your sheet or character builder. Use the exact items listed there.

## What You Are Good At

You are usually good at:

- talking, performing, persuading, and reading the room
- helping allies succeed at key rolls
- healing at clutch moments
- illusions, emotions, charm, fear, and sound
- knowing a little bit about many things
- turning a messy scene into an opportunity

## Your Core Rhythm

Bards are often strongest when they improve someone else's moment or make an enemy lose theirs.

Your default combat pattern is often:

1. Give Bardic Inspiration before an ally's important moment.
2. Use a control or support spell when the scene needs a swing.
3. Use a cantrip or weapon when you are saving resources.
4. Keep a healing option ready for emergencies.

## Bardic Inspiration

Bardic Inspiration is one of your signature tools. In 2024-style rules, you use a Bonus Action to give an ally an inspiration die. They can spend it later when it might turn a failed d20 roll into a success.

Use it before:

- a dangerous save
- a clutch attack
- a vital social roll
- a stealth, investigation, or escape attempt

If your sheet uses 2014 rules, ask the DM exactly when the die is spent. The spirit is the same: you make someone else's important roll better.

## Spell Picks To Understand

If these are on your sheet, here is when to think about using them:

| Spell | Use It When... |
| --- | --- |
| Vicious Mockery | You want light damage plus making an enemy less effective |
| Healing Word | Someone drops or needs help from a distance |
| Cure Wounds | Someone needs stronger touch-range healing |
| Dissonant Whispers | You want damage plus forced movement or panic |
| Charm Person | A social scene might turn with magical friendliness |
| Faerie Fire | You want allies to hit easier and reveal hidden targets |
| Sleep or Color Spray | You want to disable weaker enemies early |
| Disguise Self | Appearance can solve the problem |
| Silent Image or Minor Illusion | Deception, distraction, or misdirection helps |

## Watch Your Bonus Action

Bardic Inspiration and Healing Word both use your Bonus Action. On a tense turn, you may need to choose between helping someone succeed and helping someone stay up.

## Ask Yourself

- Who is about to make the most important roll?
- Can I solve this with words before spending a spell?
- Is this a control moment, a healing moment, or an inspiration moment?
- Would an illusion or performance change what people believe is happening?
