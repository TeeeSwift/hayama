# Sea Glass

## Role

Sea Glass is an Anchored concept for harm made smooth by time, friction, salt,
and concealment.

Use this concept for an Anchored who seems gentle, polished, and harmless, but
whose origin is breakage.

## Natural Model

Sea glass begins as broken glass. Waves, sand, and stone grind its sharp edges
away until it becomes frosted, rounded, and touchable.

It is not healed back into what it was. It is changed into something people can
hold.

This makes Sea Glass a figure of:

- abrasion as transformation
- damage made beautiful without being undone
- sharpness hidden by long exposure
- memory softened until it becomes collectible
- the sea turning human refuse into treasure

## Campaign Use

Sea Glass should represent the dangerous comfort of aestheticized pain.

They may preserve evidence because it is beautiful, not because it is truthful.
They may turn disaster sites into memorial objects, coded keepsakes, or local
crafts that let people carry the wound without naming it.

Use Sea Glass when the campaign needs to ask:

- what happens when trauma becomes local charm
- whether smoothing a story is mercy or erasure
- who benefits when sharp history becomes pretty

## Table Motifs

- frosted shards arranged by color
- old bottles worn unreadable
- gifts that are too personal for the giver to explain
- keepsakes made from accident debris
- a voice that is soft because the edge has been sanded away
