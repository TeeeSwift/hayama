# Riptide

## Role

Riptide is an Anchored concept for hidden outward force, panic, and danger that
punishes direct resistance.

Use this concept for an Anchored who pulls people away from safety, sometimes to
save them from a worse impact.

## Natural Model

What people often call a riptide near beaches is usually a rip current: a strong
channel of water moving away from shore. It can carry swimmers seaward, and the
danger increases when people panic and fight straight against it.

Survival often requires calm, floating, and moving sideways out of the current.

This makes Riptide a figure of:

- hidden force under a normal surface
- panic making danger worse
- escape through lateral thinking
- rescue that first feels like being dragged away
- the cruelty of direct resistance

## Campaign Use

Riptide should represent crisis forces that cannot be beaten head-on.

They may pull the party away from an obvious objective, redirect them from a
dangerous site, or expose how panic turns a solvable problem deadly.

Use Riptide when the campaign needs:

- a forced relocation that is not pure hostility
- a lesson in not fighting the wrong vector
- an Anchored whose help feels like abduction

## Table Motifs

- lateral exits
- people dragged from the obvious path
- advice to stop fighting and float
- calm instructions during panic
- a current visible only because debris moves wrong
