# Current

## Role

Current is an Anchored concept for directional force, unseen transport, and the
movement beneath visible events.

Use this concept for an Anchored who understands where things are going before
anyone understands why.

## Natural Model

Ocean currents move water through patterns driven by wind, temperature, salinity,
tides, geography, and the rotation of the earth. Currents can be local or vast,
surface-level or deep, temporary or persistent.

They carry heat, nutrients, organisms, debris, and consequences.

This makes Current a figure of:

- invisible direction
- transport without intention
- deep causes under surface events
- connection across distance
- inevitability that still has channels and variations

## Campaign Use

Current should represent the underlying movement of the plot.

They may know how evidence, people, favors, memories, and divine pressure travel
through Hayama's hidden systems.

Use Current when the campaign needs:

- a character who can predict where pressure will surface next
- clues carried from one location to another
- a force that connects distant scenes without looking like agency

## Table Motifs

- maps with arrows instead of borders
- drifting objects arriving somewhere meaningful
- favors moving through indirect hands
- someone asking "where does this go?" instead of "who did this?"
- quiet confidence about future arrival
