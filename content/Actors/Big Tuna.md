# Big Tuna

## Role

Big Tuna is an Anchored concept for muscular momentum, open-water power, and the
cost of needing to keep moving.

Use this concept for an Anchored who is impressive, hard to stop, and poorly
suited to stillness.

## Natural Model

Tuna are fast, powerful open-water fish built for long-distance movement. Many
species migrate widely, maintain high activity, and occupy important positions
in marine food webs.

They are also valuable, pursued, measured, sold, and turned into prestige.

This makes Big Tuna a figure of:

- strength through motion
- status as vulnerability
- appetite and pursuit
- the exhaustion of never stopping
- large-scale movement invisible from shore

## Campaign Use

Big Tuna should represent the big operator who cannot solve delicate problems.

They may be an Anchored with reach, resources, or physical force, but their
intervention risks making the crisis more visible.

Use Big Tuna when the campaign needs:

- a powerful ally who creates wake
- a faction member too valuable to move casually
- pressure from markets, prestige, and extraction

## Table Motifs

- refrigerated delivery chains
- expensive meals, auction prices, status gifts
- a person who paces rather than sits
- blunt solutions that move too much water
- maps of routes too large for local politics
