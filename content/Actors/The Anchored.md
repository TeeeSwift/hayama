## Experiments

The Anchored have conducted numerous experiments in an attempt to escape,
stabilize, or redefine their role within Watatsumi's cycle.

These efforts are often hidden from the public and have contributed to their
reputation as secretive or manipulative.

### Divinity Separation

One major line of experimentation attempted to externalize divine essence into
physical vessels. The goal was to allow Anchored individuals to relinquish
their divinity and become fully mortal.

This resulted in a failed artifact containing unstable, concentrated divinity.
The object was deemed too dangerous to destroy and was instead buried in a
remote mountain location, where it remained inert under the barrier's
dampening effect.

### Monitoring and Intervention

The Anchored maintain a network of subtle instruments and interventions across
Hayama, including:

- Devices that detect fluctuations in divine energy or barrier instability
- Observational studies of individuals who can perceive the tide
- Controlled exposure experiments to understand resistance to reaping

These actions are often misinterpreted by the Drifted as surveillance or
experimentation on civilians.

### Memory and Perception Management

To prevent panic and uncontrolled exposure to supernatural events, the
Anchored have at times interfered with public perception, dampening awareness
of the tide and its manifestations.

This contributes to a growing belief that they are manipulating or controlling
the population.

### Current Role

Despite appearances, the Anchored are actively attempting to repair the
barrier and prevent total collapse. Their secrecy and morally ambiguous
methods have made them appear culpable in the current crisis.
