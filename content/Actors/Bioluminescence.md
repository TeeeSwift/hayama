# Bioluminescence

## Role

Bioluminescence is an Anchored concept for living signal, hidden visibility, and
light produced under darkness, disturbance, or need.

Use this concept for an Anchored who reveals danger by glowing in response to
it.

## Natural Model

Bioluminescence is light produced by living organisms through chemical
reactions. In marine life it can lure, warn, confuse, communicate, camouflage,
or startle.

Some plankton glow when disturbed, turning motion itself into visible evidence.

This makes Bioluminescence a figure of:

- signal under pressure
- beauty as alarm
- visibility that requires darkness
- disturbance made legible
- communication without speech

## Campaign Use

Bioluminescence should represent clues that shine only when something moves
through them.

They may expose invisible currents, divine traces, lies, or emotional pressure.
They may be sought by the Anchored because they make hidden systems visible.

Use Bioluminescence when the campaign needs:

- a beautiful warning sign
- evidence revealed by contact or movement
- a character whose presence makes secrets briefly visible

## Table Motifs

- blue-green flashes in drains or tidewater
- lights that respond to footsteps or lies
- glowing residue that fades before it can be sampled
- a person most legible in darkness
- warnings that are gorgeous enough to distract from danger
