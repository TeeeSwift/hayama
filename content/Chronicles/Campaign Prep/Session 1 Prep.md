# Session 1 Prep

## Purpose

Session 1 should establish Hayama as cozy, coastal, lived-in, and subtly wrong.

The players should leave with the sense that something is happening to the sea,
the town, and local memory, but without yet understanding [[Lore/The Barrier|The
Barrier]], [[Actors/Watatsumi|Watatsumi]], or [[Actors/The Anchored|The
Anchored]].

This is Act I: Misdiagnosis. The first session should create wrong but reasonable
suspects.

Use the diving class as the first contact with the impossible tide. The shoreline

All four PCs naturally pass through the cafe for unrelated ordinary reasons

- [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]] and

  regarding local shoreline concerns or civic matters.
- [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Aizawa Marin]] visits with a friend for
  parfaits and ordinary social time.
- [[Actors/Player Characters/Ryuta|Ryuta]] is present because the cafe is
  experiencing strange electrical and systems irregularities.

The reaping event interrupts ordinary daytime life rather than a suspicious or
already supernatural situation.

## Session Shape

### Movement 1: Normal Hayama, One Wrong Detail

Open with a mundane coastal activity: the diving class.

Emphasize:

- morning salt air
- routine gear checks
- gentle local familiarity
- tourists or new arrivals trying to fit in
- Marin being competent and comfortable in the water
- Ryuta being curious and out of his depth in a grounded, human way

Then introduce one wrong detail:

- the water is sharply colder in a narrow band
- the current pulls sideways against the weather
- a dive gauge reads pressure before submersion
- a participant hears bells underwater before entering the sea
- a stretch of reef appears where Marin knows there should only be sand
- an old shoreline marker is visible under the water, though no one remembers it

Keep this deniable at first.

### Movement 2: Investigation With Conflicting Explanations

After the diving disturbance, let the scene point toward several possible causes.
None should explain the whole truth.

Possible leads:

- A municipal notice connects recent coastal inspections to the shoreline
  stabilization project.
- A diver or instructor mentions that the same strange current was reported near
  the construction zone.
- Marin notices the water is wrong in a way that feels remembered rather than
  observed.
- Ryuta receives a notification, form, stamp, or intrusive thought treating him
  as an authorized interface for something official.
- A local says the sea has been acting up since the construction, then immediately
  walks the statement back.
- [[Actors/Barnacle|Barnacle]] appears nearby, quietly checking markers,
  seawalls, drainage channels, or old stone features.

### Movement 3: Nagisabashi Reaping Event

The first major public reaping event occurs at
[[Locations/Nagisabashi Cafe|Nagisabashi Cafe]].

The scene should begin warm, ordinary, and socially grounded.

Nagisabashi represents:

- daylight
- routine
- local continuity
- ordinary human life
- the version of Hayama everyone believes they live in

The reaping should violate that sense of safety.

Do not frame the event as a monster attack.

Reality quietly fails in public.

Possible progression:

- a regular customer becomes increasingly difficult to notice
- conversations skip over them
- reflections lag behind their movement
- their order remains active in the cafe system after people stop acknowledging
  them socially
- receipts continue printing their name correctly
- timestamps and security systems continue recognizing them after human memory
  begins failing
- the person's seat still appears occupied in reflections or camera footage

Each PC notices different aspects of the event.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]]

Alex notices institutional and procedural breakdown.

Official logic stops explaining the situation.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Vincent Uminashi|Vincent Uminashi]]

Vincent notices social discontinuity.

The person belongs here. The town's social memory is failing around them.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Aizawa Marin]]

Marin feels the reaping emotionally and divinely before she understands it.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Ryuta|Ryuta]]

Ryuta notices synchronization collapse.

The cafe's systems continue acknowledging someone after reality begins failing to
categorize them correctly.

## Core Clues To Seed

Use only a few at the table. Do not dump all of them.

- The disturbance is not confined to the dive site.
- Municipal infrastructure and supernatural systems are entangled.
- The shoreline stabilization project hit something older than the official
  records admit.
- The Anchored are aware of the crisis but are not ready to explain themselves.
- The Drifted may interpret the same events as Anchored wrongdoing.
- Watatsumi's activity feels patterned but not strategic.
- Infrastructure systems remember differently than people do.

## PC-Specific Triggers

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Aizawa Marin]]

- The water recognizes her incorrectly.
- A reef, charm, shell, or current responds to her as if she carries something
  misplaced.
- She can tell the dive site is wrong before anyone else has evidence.
- Her father may warn her: "Water feels different today."

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Ryuta|Ryuta]]

- A divine/civic system treats him as authorized.
- He receives a notice, voice, stamp, or pressure sensation meant for another
  interface.
- The connection should feel bureaucratic, accidental, and intermittent rather
  than chosen or prophetic.
- Nagisabashi's systems continue acknowledging someone after social recognition
  begins failing.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]]

- Her approval of the shoreline project should surface early as a mundane paper
  trail.
- The implication should be uncomfortable but not accusatory yet.
- Her pearl from [[Actors/Mollusk|Mollusk]] may resonate with sea-borne or
  barrier-adjacent phenomena.

### [[Actors/Player Characters/Vincent Uminashi|Vincent Uminashi]]

- Locals recognize him or his family name.
- He notices a gap in local continuity: a person, practice, shoreline feature, or
  memory that should be stable is not.
- [[Actors/Chizuru|Chizuru]] can appear at the edge of the scene if a quiet,
  watchful reminder of lineage is useful.

## NPC Bench

These NPCs are intended as ready-to-use table pieces. Promote only the ones who
become useful. Avoid canonizing all of them as major figures immediately.

### Civic / Project NPCs

#### Kuroda Emi — Municipal Coastal Works Liaison

- Public role: friendly city representative handling resident questions about
  the shoreline stabilization project
- Table use: provides official explanations, schedules inspections, deflects
  blame from [[Actors/The Mayor|The Mayor]]
- Surface: efficient, apologetic, overprepared
- Secret / pressure: knows the project struck an undocumented stone structure but
  believes hiding that detail prevents panic and lawsuits
- Voice: "Let's not turn an engineering issue into a ghost story before we've
  checked the paperwork."

#### Hasegawa Toru — Site Foreman

- Public role: contractor who supervised work near the damaged anchor point
- Table use: practical witness who saw something weird before it was buried or
  removed
- Surface: tired, blunt, protective of his crew
- Secret / pressure: one worker quit after hearing waves underground
- Voice: "Concrete cracks. Rebar sings. Ground settles. But stone does not bleed
  saltwater."

#### Nishimura Saki — Junior Records Clerk

- Public role: municipal office clerk managing permits, old maps, and inspection
  forms
- Table use: access point for paperwork, maps, approvals, and Ryuta's erroneous
  registration
- Surface: anxious, sharp, too aware of procedural mistakes
- Secret / pressure: has seen records rewrite themselves overnight
- Voice: "I know what I filed. I also know that's not what the archive says now."

### Diving / Coastal NPCs

#### Moriyama Keisuke — Dive Instructor

- Public role: lead instructor for the Hayama diving class
- Table use: grounds the opening scene and keeps civilians organized during panic
- Surface: calm, sun-weathered, safety-focused
- Secret / pressure: has been diving here for decades and knows the reef that
  appeared should not exist
- Voice: "The sea gets moods. This wasn't a mood."

#### Tanabe Riko — First-Time Diver

- Public role: nervous participant in the diving class
- Table use: endangered civilian, panic vector, or sympathetic witness
- Surface: chatty, embarrassed by fear, eager to seem brave
- Secret / pressure: heard someone call her name underwater before she entered
- Voice: "I thought it was one of you joking. But it sounded like my grandmother."

#### Oda Fumi — Retired Ama Diver

- Public role: elderly local shellfish diver watching from shore
- Table use: old local knowledge without full exposition
- Surface: dry humor, sharp eyes, unimpressed by officials
- Secret / pressure: recognizes signs of an older shoreline returning
- Voice: "Young people call it erosion when the sea remembers where it slept."

### Local Community NPCs

#### Kitamura Haru — Beachside Cafe Owner

- Public role: runs a small cafe near the dive site
- Table use: cozy anchor, rumor hub, place to regroup after the incident
- Surface: warm, gossipy, generous with food
- Secret / pressure: keeps serving regulars no one else remembers afterward
- Voice: "Eat first. Panic after. Panic on an empty stomach makes people stupid."

#### Fujino Masato — Local Fisherman

- Public role: small-boat fisherman with opinions about the project
- Table use: gives grounded sea-lore, complains about civic meddling
- Surface: suspicious, observant, practical
- Secret / pressure: his nets recently pulled up shrine rope and municipal survey
  flags tangled together
- Voice: "They keep drawing lines on maps. The sea keeps taking them personally."

#### Arai Chika — Middle School Student

- Public role: local kid skipping errands near the coast
- Table use: witness to impossible details adults rationalize away
- Surface: bold, curious, too online, not easily frightened
- Secret / pressure: drew a map of Hayama with several streets underwater before
  the session begins
- Voice: "Adults only call things impossible when they don't want to do paperwork."

### Suspicious / Supernatural-Adjacent NPCs

#### [[Actors/Barnacle|Barnacle]]

- Public role: local maintenance contractor quietly checking seawalls, drainage
  channels, shrine edges, and old infrastructure
- Table use: suspicious recurring presence who is actually monitoring barrier
  failure
- Surface: polite, evasive, physically still
- Secret / pressure: knows the disturbances are symptoms, not causes
- Voice: "Do not touch what the tide leaves behind unless you are prepared to be
  noticed."

#### Iwase Ren — Drifted Agitator

- Public role: local activist blaming old powers and civic secrecy for coastal
  anomalies
- Table use: gives the party a plausible anti-Anchored interpretation
- Surface: intense, persuasive, wounded
- Secret / pressure: right that something is being hidden, wrong about the full
  shape of the crisis
- Voice: "Every time something vanishes, someone powerful calls it maintenance."

#### Suzume — Shrine Helper With Missing Context

- Public role: young helper from a small coastal shrine
- Table use: ritual texture, partial warnings, soft supernatural sensitivity
- Surface: gentle, formal, easily overwhelmed
- Secret / pressure: performs small rites she does not fully understand because
  the previous caretaker disappeared from local memory
- Voice: "We leave shells for safe return. Lately, something has been returning
  them unopened."

## Reaping Event Toolbox

Use these as modular beats.

### Sensory Signs

- salt smell before any water appears
- pressure in the ears
- gulls going silent at once
- light bending like it is underwater
- wet footprints leading away from dry sand
- bells, whale song, or distant temple tones beneath ordinary noise

### Physical Manifestations

- barnacles on fresh concrete
- tide pools in asphalt cracks
- seaweed tangled in municipal fencing
- old shells embedded in new equipment
- water running uphill toward a person or object
- maps and signs showing a slightly older coastline

### Memory Effects

- witnesses agree something happened but disagree who was present
- a victim's name is briefly hard to say
- a photo shows one extra wet figure in the background
- a roll call has a blank line everyone skips over
- locals rationalize the impossible as ordinary coastal inconvenience
- infrastructure systems continue acknowledging someone after people stop doing
  so

## Likely Ending States

### Clean Save

The party saves the endangered person and recovers a clue. They gain trust from
one or two locals, but officials begin managing the narrative quickly.

### Messy Save

The party saves the person but exposes something publicly impossible. The Mayor's
office or municipal staff become interested in controlling the story.

### Missed Clue

The manifestation ends without a clear object recovered. Use NPC testimony,
changed records, or Ryuta's misdirected authorization to keep the investigation
moving.

### Someone Is Partially Taken

A victim survives but comes back wrong: wet hair that never dries, inability to
remember the current shoreline, or a new fear of being named aloud near water.

## What Not To Reveal Yet

Do not explain:

- The Anchored built the Barrier.
- Watatsumi is following a natural cycle rather than attacking strategically.
- The Barrier also dampens unstable divinity.
- [[Lore/The Artifact|The Artifact]] is leaking in the mountains.
- The full Zenji / Teru / Marin divinity chain.
- Ryuta's exact divine-system error.

Do show:

- the sea is behaving like a system
- civic records are implicated
- old local knowledge matters
- divine identity is unstable
- the Anchored are hiding something
- the town's permanence may be unnatural
- infrastructure remembers differently than people do

## Prep Checklist

Before running, choose:

- one endangered civilian
- one physical clue left by the tide
- one municipal lead pointing toward the shoreline project
- one suspicious Anchored sighting
- one PC-specific trigger for each present player
