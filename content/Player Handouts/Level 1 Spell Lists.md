# Level 1 Spell Lists

Use this as a spell menu for level 1 characters. It lists cantrips and level 1
spells from the 2024 D&D Basic Rules for bards, druids, rangers, and warlocks.

Special tags:

- C = Concentration
- R = Ritual
- M = specific Material component

## Bard

At level 1, a bard knows two cantrips and prepares four level 1 bard spells.
Bards regain spell slots after a Long Rest. In 2024 rules, bards usually change
prepared level 1+ spells when they gain a bard level, not after every Long Rest.

## Ranger

At level 1, a ranger prepares two level 1 ranger spells and has two level 1
spell slots. Hunter's Mark is always prepared and can be cast without spending a
spell slot twice per Long Rest.

Rangers do not normally know cantrips at level 1. At level 2, a ranger who
chooses the Druidic Warrior Fighting Style learns two druid cantrips; use the
Druid cantrip list above for those options.

### Ranger Cantrips

| Source | Available Cantrips |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 ranger | None by default |
| Level 2 Druidic Warrior Fighting Style | Choose two druid cantrips |

## Warlock

At level 1, a warlock knows two cantrips, prepares two level 1 warlock spells,
has one Pact Magic spell slot, and chooses one Eldritch Invocation. Warlock Pact
Magic spell slots return after a Short or Long Rest.

Important 2024 note: Agonizing Blast is not a level 1 invocation in the 2024
Basic Rules. It unlocks at Warlock level 2+.

### Eldritch Invocations

At level 1, a warlock chooses one invocation. These 2024 Basic Rules invocations
are available at level 1:

| Invocation | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| Armor of Shadows | Cast Mage Armor on yourself without spending a spell slot |
| Eldritch Mind | Advantage on Constitution saves to maintain Concentration |
| Pact of the Blade | Conjure or bind a pact weapon and use Charisma with it |
| Pact of the Chain | Learn Find Familiar and gain special familiar options |
| Pact of the Tome | Gain a Book of Shadows with extra cantrips and rituals |

These unlock at warlock level 2:

| Invocation | Prerequisite | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agonizing Blast | Level 2+, damaging warlock cantrip | Add Charisma modifier to that cantrip's damage |
| Devil's Sight | Level 2+ | See normally in magical and nonmagical darkness |
| Eldritch Spear | Level 2+, damaging ranged cantrip | Greatly increase that cantrip's range |
| Fiendish Vigor | Level 2+ | Cast False Life on yourself without a slot |
| Lessons of the First Ones | Level 2+ | Gain an Origin feat |
| Mask of Many Faces | Level 2+ | Cast Disguise Self without a slot |
| Misty Visions | Level 2+ | Cast Silent Image without a slot |
| Otherworldly Leap | Level 2+ | Cast Jump on yourself without a slot |
| Repelling Blast | Level 2+, attack-roll warlock cantrip | Push a hit creature away with that cantrip |

Higher-level eldritch options:

| Invocation | Prerequisite | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ascendant Step | Level 5+ | Cast Levitate on yourself without a slot |
| Eldritch Smite | Level 5+, Pact of the Blade | Spend a Pact Magic slot for extra force damage and knockdown |
| Gaze of Two Minds | Level 5+ | Perceive through a willing creature and cast from either position |
| Gift of the Depths | Level 5+ | Breathe underwater, gain swim speed, and cast Water Breathing |
| Investment of the Chain Master | Level 5+, Pact of the Chain | Improve your familiar's movement, attacks, damage, saves, and defense |
| Master of Myriad Forms | Level 5+ | Cast Alter Self without a slot |
| One with Shadows | Level 5+ | Cast Invisibility on yourself in dim light or darkness |
| Thirsting Blade | Level 5+, Pact of the Blade | Attack twice with your pact weapon |
| Whispers of the Grave | Level 7+ | Cast Speak with Dead without a slot |
| Gift of the Protectors | Level 9+, Pact of the Tome | Book of Shadows can protect named creatures from dropping to 0 HP |
| Lifedrinker | Level 9+, Pact of the Blade | Add extra damage and spend Hit Dice to heal |
| Visions of Distant Realms | Level 9+ | Cast Arcane Eye without a slot |
| Devouring Blade | Level 12+, Thirsting Blade | Gain a third pact-weapon attack |
| Witch Sight | Level 15+ | Gain Truesight out to 30 feet |

### Fathomless Patron Note

The Fathomless patron comes from _Tasha's Cauldron of Everything_. If your sheet
uses the older Fathomless rules, your patron features may appear at level 1. If
your sheet uses the 2024 warlock chassis, subclass features usually begin at
level 3. Use the sheet at the table.

If using the Fathomless expanded spell list, these level 1 spells are added to
that warlock's available spell options:
