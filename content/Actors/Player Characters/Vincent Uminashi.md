# Vincent Uminashi

## Elevator Pitch

A wealthy local heir from an old Hayama family, tied to a long line of Drifted
blood and a quiet, persistent connection that has followed his lineage for
generations. He manages properties and development relationships using his
father's money and his mother's local ties, placing him at the intersection of
modern civic growth and Hayama's older patterns.

## Surface Identity

- Background: Folk Hero; local, affluent family
- Profession: Property manager and development liaison
- Disposition: Grounded, capable, accustomed to stability

Vincent Uminashi oversees properties connected to his family and helps
coordinate maintenance, access, contractors, and development relationships
around town.

He operates through a combination of:

- his father's capital
- his mother's local family connections
- his own familiarity with Hayama and its people

The town is not strange to him. It is home.

---

## Hidden Significance

Vincent Uminashi is a descendant of [[Actors/The Drifted|The Drifted]] through his
mother's lineage, part of a bloodline that traces back to the earliest
generations of Hayama.

This lineage carries a subtle but persistent connection to divine patterns,
though Vincent Uminashi is not consciously aware of it.

His work also places him near one of the campaign's inciting events: a
city-funded shoreline stabilization initiative near the coast. Because Vincent
Uminashi helps manage land access, contractors, and local relationships around
projects like this, he is positioned to notice patterns that others treat as
isolated construction problems.

## The Housemaid

Vincent Uminashi's household has long employed a housemaid who has served the
family across generations.

She appears entirely normal:

- composed
- professional
- unremarkable in demeanor

Vincent Uminashi is aware of a rumor:

- that her father was responsible for burning the royal villa in town

This rumor has never been confirmed, but it lingers as a quiet mark against her
otherwise impeccable presence.

---

## True Nature (Hidden)

The housemaid is [[Actors/Chizuru|Chizuru]], one of
[[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]].

She embodies one half of a phenomenon often described as the miracle of deep
sea creatures finding one another in the dark.

Long ago, she was in love with one of Vincent Uminashi's ancestors. As that
ancestor began to fade into mortality, they made a promise:

> that she would find her again, somehow, through her descendants.

Chizuru knows miracles are not guaranteed and that identity does not simply
persist unchanged across generations. She remains near the bloodline anyway.

---

## Relationship to Chizuru

Vincent Uminashi is not the same person as his ancestor, but he carries a similar
pattern.

Chizuru does not treat him as a replacement.

Instead:

- she observes
- she remains present
- she waits

Her connection to him is:

- patient
- unresolved
- shaped more by time than by expectation

---

## Thematic Role

Vincent Uminashi represents:

- Continuity across generations
- Inheritance without understanding
- The persistence of connection over time
- The meeting point between civic modernity and older hidden structures

He contrasts with figures like [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]], who embody
transformation and change.

---

## Campaign Role

- Early: Grounded local perspective within a destabilizing town
- Mid: Recognition that recurring problems across multiple sites may be part of
  a larger pattern
- Late: Confrontation with a relationship and inheritance that have outlived
  their original form

His choices may determine whether inherited patterns are preserved, broken, or
redefined.
