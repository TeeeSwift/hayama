# Bard Level 1 Spells

Class guide: [[Player Handouts/Class Guides/Bard|Bard]]

Use this as a spell menu for a level 1 bard. It lists cantrips and level 1 spells from the 2024 D&D Basic Rules.

Special tags:

- C = Concentration
- R = Ritual
- M = specific Material component

At level 1, a bard knows two cantrips and prepares four level 1 bard spells. Bards regain spell slots after a Long Rest. In 2024 rules, bards usually change prepared level 1+ spells when they gain a bard level, not after every Long Rest.

Damage and healing notes use your bard spellcasting ability, Charisma. If a spell says "save," the target rolls against your Spell Save DC. If a spell says "spell attack," you roll with your Spell Attack Bonus.

| Friends | Enchantment | C | Gives magical social pressure against one creature for a brief conversation. No damage; risky if the target realizes magic was involved. |
| Light | Evocation |  | Makes one object shine bright light. No damage; useful for exploration and marking objects. |
| Mage Hand | Conjuration |  | Creates a floating hand that manipulates unattended objects at range. No damage; useful for levers, doors, keys, and safe interaction. |

| Message | Transmutation |  | Sends a whispered message to a creature and lets it whisper back. No damage; useful for stealth and coordination. |
| Minor Illusion | Illusion |  | Creates a small sound or image. No damage; useful for distractions, hiding, fake cover, and misdirection. |

| True Strike | Divination |  | Make one weapon attack using your spellcasting ability. On a hit, the attack uses the weapon's normal damage and can turn the damage radiant. |
| Vicious Mockery | Enchantment |  | Target makes a Wisdom save. Failed save takes `1d6` psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the spell ends. |
