# Sato-san

An elderly regular at the diving school who joined
[[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Marin]]'s beginner lesson in
[[Chronicles/Session 1/index|Session 1]].

During the dive, a sudden impossible current knocked Sato-san's regulator loose.
He survived, reassured Marin afterward, and said he would return.
