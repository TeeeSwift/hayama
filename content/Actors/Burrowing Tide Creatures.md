# Burrowing Tide Creatures

## Role

Burrowing Tide Creatures is an Anchored concept for withdrawal, concealment, and
survival by disappearing into the substrate.

Use this concept for an Anchored who hides below ordinary attention until the
conditions are safe.

## Natural Model

Many shoreline creatures bury themselves in sand or mud when the tide changes.
Clams, small crabs, worms, and other animals use burrowing to avoid drying out,
predators, impact, and exposure.

The surface may look empty while life remains active underneath.

This makes Burrowing Tide Creatures a figure of:

- hidden life beneath blank surfaces
- retreat as intelligence
- survival through stillness
- evidence visible only as holes, bubbles, or disturbed sand
- the difference between absence and concealment

## Campaign Use

This concept should represent hidden witnesses, buried systems, and the town's
habit of surviving by staying out of sight.

They may know how to hide people, records, or divine traces in plain ground.
They may resist the party not because they are hostile, but because exposure
itself is dangerous.

Use Burrowing Tide Creatures when the campaign needs:

- a concealed route under a public place
- evidence that something withdrew moments before discovery
- an Anchored who treats being unseen as care

## Table Motifs

- pinholes in damp sand or concrete dust
- bubbles rising from mud or drains
- records stored under floors
- people who stop speaking when the room gets too bright
- survival instructions that begin with "get low"
