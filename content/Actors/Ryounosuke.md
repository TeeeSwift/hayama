Child god and literal incarnation of Rain and Storms.

His temperament is chaotic and can manifest as anything from a cool breeze, to a raging storm.

He is an anomaly, in that he combines 2 lines of divinitiy.

On his father's side, he is one of [[The Drifted]].
On his mother's side, he inherits divinity of the mountain. 

Combining the fluid, rhythmic nature of The Drifted with the ancient, grounded
power of the Mountains, he isn't just a "storm god," but a cycle of nature itself.

Mountains force air upwards, cooling it and squeezing out moisture as rain. 
This is called the Orographic Effect and represents his literal birth.

- The physical manifestation of his father (the ocean/moist air) "colliding" with his mother (the stone peaks).
- In calm, he is the Blue Ridge Mist. That hazy, ethereal smoke that clings to Appalachian hollows.
- In chaos, he is a Thunderhead anchored to a single peak.

**The Veins of the Mountain:** He represents the transition from salt to fresh. As a
child of the Drifted, his blood might be briny, but as he touches his mother’s
stone, it filters through the "roots" of the mountain and emerges as
crystal-clear springs.

**The Headwaters:** He is the "Lord of the Headwaters." His presence
could cause dry creek beds to suddenly roar with life (flash floods) or create
"balds"—high altitude meadows kept lush by his constant drizzle.

He embodies the dirty chaos and brine of the sea, and the pure life-giving magic of deep mountains.
All that could be, all that is, and all that will be.
He is temperamental, uncontrollable, fair, and ruthless.
