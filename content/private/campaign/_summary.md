# Campaign Summary

## Premise

The campaign is a magical version of [[Atlas/Hayama|Hayama]], where the town's
existence depends on an ancient pact with the sea god [[Actors/Watatsumi|Watatsumi]].
The land and many of its people ultimately trace back to that divine arrangement.

The core threat is the approaching [[Lore/The Beckoning Tide|Beckoning Tide]]:
Watatsumi reclaiming what was granted through [[Lore/The Salt Lease|The Salt Lease]].

## Canon Spine

- [[Lore/The Salt Lease|The Salt Lease]]: Hayama was raised from the sea by Watatsumi so coastal spirits could live mortal lives.
- [[Actors/The Morning Tide|The Morning Tide]]: the original lesser gods who accepted mortality.
- [[Actors/The Drifted|The Drifted]]: descendants who chose ordinary mortal life and mostly lost touch with the divine pact.
- [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]]: the few who remained loyal or connected to Watatsumi across the lease.
- [[Lore/The Beckoning Tide|The Beckoning Tide]]: the staged reclaiming of Hayama if the lease expires.

## Active Story Tension

- The town is nearing the end of the thousand-year lease.
- Most present-day residents do not understand the true origin of Hayama.
- Some Anchored and restored divine figures know the danger is real.
- The campaign note frames the story around an "Interspecies Wellness Center," outsider newcomers, strange infrastructure pressure, coral in the sewers, and a sense that locals are missing obvious omens.

## Important Powers And Groups

- [[Actors/Watatsumi|Watatsumi]]: major sea god, patron deity of Hayama, cycles through twelve moon-linked forms plus a rare blue-moon thirteenth form.
- [[Actors/The Morning Tide|The Morning Tide]]: founding divine cohort behind the mortal experiment.
- [[Actors/The Drifted|The Drifted]]: mortal descendants carrying diluted divine traces.
- [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]]: loyal holdouts still tied to the original bargain.
- [[Actors/The Mayor|The Mayor]]: octopus deity, current mayor of Hayama, an Anchored power broker who helps other Anchored fake deaths and identity changes.
- [[Actors/Mollusk|Mollusk]]: oyster deity tied to transformation, purification, and turning pain into beauty.

## Known NPC And PC Relationships

- [[Actors/Ryuta|Ryuta]] is a warlock whose patron is [[Actors/The Mayor|The Mayor]].
- [[Actors/Taylor|Taylor]] and [[Actors/Sen|Sen]] are connected to [[Actors/Ryounosuke|Ryounosuke]], the child storm-and-mountain god.
- [[Actors/Ryounosuke|Ryounosuke]] is the child of Drifted sea lineage and mountain divinity.
- [[Actors/Zenji|Zenji]] is a resurrected founding member of [[Actors/The Morning Tide|The Morning Tide]].
- [[Actors/Teru|Teru]] accidentally reanimated [[Actors/Zenji|Zenji]] and now knows too much.
- [[Actors/Pidgeon Gods|Pidgeon Gods]] are a trio of divine troublemakers associated with [[Atlas/Nango Park|Nango Park]].
