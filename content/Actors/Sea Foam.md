# Sea Foam

## Role

Sea Foam is an Anchored concept for unstable boundaries, brief forms, and things
that appear solid only because motion holds them together.

Use this concept for an Anchored whose presence is obvious in the moment and
nearly impossible to prove afterward.

## Natural Model

Sea foam forms when seawater is churned with air and organic matter. Waves,
wind, and turbulence make bubbles gather into pale, shifting masses that can
cover rock, sand, drains, or seawalls before collapsing.

It is real, but temporary. It has shape, but not permanence.

This makes Sea Foam a figure of:

- temporary manifestation
- noisy evidence that leaves little trace
- boundaries made from agitation
- beauty born from disturbance
- a surface that conceals depth

## Campaign Use

Sea Foam should represent the parts of the crisis that become visible only under
pressure.

They may appear during public panic, media attention, civic meetings, or
supernatural surges, then vanish before anyone can document them cleanly.

Use Sea Foam when the campaign needs:

- a witness whose testimony evaporates
- an event that was visible to everyone but recorded by no one
- a sign that turbulence is making hidden material surface

## Table Motifs

- suds in places without soap
- phone videos that show only glare and white noise
- crowds repeating contradictory versions of the same event
- pale residue that vanishes when touched
- a voice that talks quickly because the moment will not last
