# Fathomless Warlock Reference

This page is a paraphrased table reference for the Fathomless patron from _Tasha's Cauldron of Everything_. Use the character sheet as the source of truth at the table.

Rules version note: this page uses the older Fathomless patron timing, where patron features can appear at warlock level 1. If your sheet uses the 2024 warlock chassis, subclass timing may be different.

## Core Theme

A Fathomless warlock is tied to a deep-water patron: an ancient sea power, a monster below the waves, a water-plane entity, a sea hag coven, a merfolk demigod, a kraken-like intelligence, or something stranger that communicates through drowning dreams, pressure, salt, cold, and impossible currents.

Mechanically, the subclass is about:

- Bonus Action pressure through a spectral tentacle
- water breathing and fast swimming
- cold resistance and underwater communication later
- protecting allies near the tentacle
- battlefield control through water, storm, cold, and tentacle spells
- emergency water-linked teleportation at high level

## Level 1 Features

| Feature | Action | Uses | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tentacle of the Deeps | Bonus Action | Proficiency bonus per Long Rest | Create a spectral tentacle at a visible point within 60 feet. It lasts 1 minute. When it appears, make a melee spell attack from the tentacle against one creature within 10 feet of it. On a hit, deal `1d8` cold damage and reduce the target's speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. On later turns, use a Bonus Action to move the tentacle up to 30 feet and make the attack again. |
| Gift of the Sea | Always on | Unlimited | You can breathe underwater and have a 40-foot swimming speed. This does not use an action, spell slot, or Concentration. |

For Ryunnu at level 1:

- Tentacle attack uses his spell attack bonus.
- With proficiency bonus +2, he can create the tentacle 2 times per Long Rest.
- The tentacle does not spend his Pact Magic spell slot.
- The tentacle does not require Concentration.

## Expanded Spell List

These spells are added to the Fathomless warlock's available warlock spell options. Under the 2014 warlock rules, this means they are options you can choose when you learn warlock spells; they are not automatically known unless the sheet says otherwise.

| Spell Level | Warlock Level | Spells | Use Them For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 1 | Create or Destroy Water, Thunderwave | water utility, evidence, survival, fire problems, close-range shove/damage |
| 2nd | 3 | Gust of Wind, Silence | positioning, fog/gas pressure, shutting down sound or spellcasting |
| 3rd | 5 | Lightning Bolt, Sleet Storm | line damage, large-area disruption, anti-Concentration pressure |
| 4th | 7 | Control Water, Summon Elemental | major water manipulation, calling a water elemental-style ally |
| 5th | 9 | Bigby's Hand, Cone of Cold | large magical tentacle/hand control, strong cold-area damage |

Bigby's Hand can be described as a spectral tentacle for this patron. That changes the appearance, not the spell's basic mechanics.

## Level 6 Features

| Feature | Action | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oceanic Soul | Always on | You gain resistance to cold damage. While fully underwater, you can understand any other fully submerged creature, and they can understand you. |
| Guardian Coil | Reaction | When you or a creature you can see takes damage within 10 feet of your Tentacle of the Deeps, use your Reaction to reduce the damage to one such creature by `1d8`. At warlock level 10, this reduction becomes `2d8`. |

Reaction conflict: Guardian Coil competes with reaction spells such as Hellish Rebuke. You only get one Reaction between your turns.

## Level 10 Feature

| Feature | Action | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grasping Tentacles | Spell action, when casting the spell | You learn Evard's Black Tentacles as a warlock spell, and it does not count against your number of warlock spells known. You can cast it once without a spell slot per Long Rest. When you cast it, you gain temporary HP equal to your warlock level, and damage cannot break your Concentration on that spell. |

Important Concentration note: damage cannot break Concentration on the special Evard's Black Tentacles casting from this feature, but other Concentration rules still matter. For example, you still cannot concentrate on two spells at once.

Tentacle scaling: at level 10, Tentacle of the Deeps deals `2d8` cold damage instead of `1d8`.

## Level 14 Feature

| Feature | Action | Recharge | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fathomless Plunge | Action | Short or Long Rest | Teleport yourself and up to five willing creatures you can see within 30 feet. You reappear up to 1 mile away in or near a body of water you have seen that is at least pond-sized. |

This is an escape, infiltration, travel, or rescue feature. It is strongest when the party has been paying attention to nearby ponds, rivers, coves, harbors, shrine pools, drainage canals, or sea access.

## Action Economy

Fathomless warlocks have several things competing for the same turn resources.

| Resource | Common Fathomless Uses |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Eldritch Blast, leveled spells, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Search |
| Bonus Action | Tentacle of the Deeps, Hex, Expeditious Retreat, Pact of the Blade weapon conjuring |
| Reaction | Hellish Rebuke, opportunity attacks, Guardian Coil at level 6 |
| Concentration | Hex, Bane, Expeditious Retreat, Gust of Wind, Silence, Sleet Storm, Evard's Black Tentacles |

At low levels, the big choice is often:

- use Hex if you expect to keep attacking the same target for several rounds
- use Tentacle of the Deeps if you want damage plus speed control without spending a spell slot
- save your Bonus Action if another feature or spell needs it this turn

## Table Rulings To Remember

- Tentacle of the Deeps is a class feature, not a spell.
- The tentacle makes a melee spell attack, so use the warlock's spell attack bonus.
- The tentacle is not a normal creature with its own turn.
- The tentacle does not automatically make opportunity attacks.
- The tentacle is best treated as a magical effect at a point in space, not as an ally, unless the DM says otherwise.
- You can only use one Bonus Action on your turn.
- You can only take one Reaction before the start of your next turn.
- The tentacle's slow effect helps with kiting, blocking retreats, stopping enemies from reaching fragile allies, and making difficult terrain more punishing.

## Hayama Flavor Hooks

For this campaign, a Fathomless warlock can be flavored without changing mechanics:

- tentacle as black seawater, kelp, rope, pale coral, shadowed foam, shrine cord, or deep-pressure distortion
- Gift of the Sea as lungs remembering salt water
- Oceanic Soul as voices becoming clear beneath the surface
- Guardian Coil as the sea placing itself between harm and the chosen
- Grasping Tentacles as the old tide reaching up through streets, drains, tatami shadows, or harbor water
