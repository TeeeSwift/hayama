A town in Japan


The town of Hayama has a unique and mystical history, deeply intertwined with
the sea and the great sea god, Watatsumi. According to local lore, the original
inhabitants of Hayama were not human but nature spirits. These spirits yearned
to experience mortal life, and Watatsumi granted their wish through a pact known
as "The Salt Lease."

This agreement allowed the spirits to assume human form and live on land that
Watatsumi himself raised from the ocean depths. For a thousand years, the people
of Hayama have prospered, their lives and deaths tied to the sea. However, this
pact was not a permanent gift; it was a lease, and the term is nearing its end.
The town now faces an existential threat as the sea prepares to reclaim the land
and the people's very forms are at risk of dissolving back into the brine.

The fate of Hayama rests on the town's ability to navigate the terms of the
lease, which offers three potential paths for renewal: a profound sacrifice, the
recovery of a lost divine artifact, or a trial to prove that the people of
Hayama have transcended their spiritual origins and become a new race entirely.

Watatsumi, the town's powerful and mercurial patron, is a key figure in this
ongoing saga. The god cycles through twelve different forms with the changing
moons, and even has a thirteenth, unpredictable form that appears during a blue
moon. The town's destiny is inextricably linked to the whims of this powerful
sea deity.
