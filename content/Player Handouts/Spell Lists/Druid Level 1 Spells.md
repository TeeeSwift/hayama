# Druid Level 1 Spells

Class guide: [[Player Handouts/Class Guides/Druid|Druid]]

Use this as a spell menu for a level 1 druid. It lists cantrips and level 1 spells from the 2024 D&D Basic Rules.

Special tags:

- C = Concentration
- R = Ritual
- M = specific Material component

At level 1, a druid knows two cantrips and prepares four level 1 druid spells. Druids regain spell slots after a Long Rest and can change prepared level 1+ spells after a Long Rest. In 2024 rules, Speak with Animals is always prepared from the Druidic feature.
