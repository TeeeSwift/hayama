# Light Patterns On Water

## Role

Light Patterns On Water is an Anchored concept for perception, misdirection,
and truth that exists as movement rather than object.

Use this concept for an Anchored who reveals patterns without ever giving the
party something stable to hold.

## Natural Model

Sunlight on moving water creates caustic patterns: shifting nets, flashes, and
ripples of brightness caused by refraction and reflection. The pattern is real,
but it depends on surface, light, angle, and motion.

Look directly and it changes. Look away and it may become clearer.

This makes Light Patterns On Water a figure of:

- truth as perception
- beauty that misleads and reveals
- signals that require angle
- the impossibility of freezing a living pattern
- surface motion exposing hidden shape

## Campaign Use

This concept should represent clues that cannot be understood as static facts.

They may communicate through reflections, timing, room arrangement, projected
images, or discrepancies between what people saw from different angles.

Use Light Patterns On Water when the campaign needs:

- a clue that only appears in motion
- multiple witnesses who are all partially right
- a person who teaches the party how to look instead of what to think

## Table Motifs

- flicker across ceilings
- reflections that briefly align with maps
- glare obscuring a face in a photo
- clues visible only from a specific position
- someone who answers by adjusting blinds, water, or mirrors
