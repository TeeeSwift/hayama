## Current Core Mystery

The [[Atlas/Uminashi Seawall|Uminashi seawall]] disrupted the wave rhythm needed
by a coastal [[Lore/The Barrier|Barrier]] anchor. The failure has restarted
fragmentary reclaiming events, weakened the Barrier's divinity dampening, caused
the [[Lore/The Artifact|Artifact]] to leak in the mountains, and forced
[[Actors/The Anchored|the Anchored]] into increasingly visible containment work.
