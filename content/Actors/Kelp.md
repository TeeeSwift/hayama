# Kelp

## Role

Kelp is an Anchored concept for rooted flexibility, underwater forests, and
systems that shelter many lives by bending instead of resisting.

Use this concept for an Anchored who provides habitat, cover, and entanglement.

## Natural Model

Kelp are large brown algae that grow in underwater forests. They anchor to rock
with holdfasts and extend upward through the water, moving with waves and
currents rather than standing rigidly against them.

Kelp forests shelter many species, dampen force, and create complex habitat.

This makes Kelp a figure of:

- flexibility rooted in place
- shelter through density
- entanglement as protection
- forests hidden below the surface
- strength that bends

## Campaign Use

Kelp should represent networks that protect by surrounding and slowing.

They may provide safehouses, information cover, social camouflage, or confusing
routes through ordinary civic life.

Use Kelp when the campaign needs:

- protection that is hard to navigate
- a route that slows pursuit without direct confrontation
- an Anchored whose help feels like being wrapped in many obligations

## Table Motifs

- tangled ropes, cables, curtains, hanging plants
- green-brown shadows under water
- safe routes that are indirect
- someone who blocks danger by yielding around it
- help that leaves the party gently entangled
