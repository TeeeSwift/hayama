Oyster deity. Publicly, she is Beatrice: Alex's landlord and the owner of the
restaurant below Alex's apartment.

Is ready to return to the sea.

Characteristics of Oysters

Pearl is based on irritants that they can't eject. They symbolize taking
something bad and making it beautiful. Turning pain into a pearl. They are also
bio-filter purifiers. They literally breathe the water around them to clean it.

As a mortal, this manifests in taking life experiences and turning it into something beautiful.

Hands a pearl to [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]], which
activates and gives her bard powers once [[Lore/The Barrier|the city's divinity
dampener]] is broken.
