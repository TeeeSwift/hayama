# Surf And Waves

## Role

Surf And Waves is an Anchored concept for repeated force, play, rhythm, and
impact that becomes structure through repetition.

Use this concept for an Anchored who treats danger as timing, sport, practice,
or improvisation.

## Natural Model

Surf forms where waves break near shore. Energy travels across water, meets
shallows or obstacles, and collapses into foam, sound, current, and impact.

Waves are repetitive but never identical. They erode, deliver, cleanse, injure,
and invite play.

This makes Surf And Waves a figure of:

- rhythm under chaos
- force that arrives in sets
- play at the edge of danger
- erosion through repetition
- the sea practicing the same question until land answers

## Campaign Use

Surf And Waves should represent pressure that comes in pulses.

They may understand the timing of reclaiming events, public concern, or Barrier
stress better than anyone who looks only for single causes.

Use Surf And Waves when the campaign needs:

- recurring encounters with changing intensity
- a character who reads rhythm instead of motive
- a reminder that play can still be dangerous

## Table Motifs

- counting under their breath
- arriving on the third repetition of a problem
- knowing when a crowd will break
- wet footprints in repeating intervals
- advice framed as timing: wait, duck, move, breathe
