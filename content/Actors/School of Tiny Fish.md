# School Of Tiny Fish

## Role

School Of Tiny Fish is an Anchored concept for collective perception, quick
avoidance, and safety through synchronized motion.

Use this concept for an Anchored who is not powerful alone but is almost
impossible to catch as part of a group.

## Natural Model

Small fish often school to confuse predators, share awareness, conserve energy,
and respond quickly to danger. A school can turn as if it were one body, though
it is made of many individuals following local signals.

The intelligence is distributed.

This makes School Of Tiny Fish a figure of:

- many small decisions becoming one motion
- safety through coordination
- information moving faster than command
- anonymity inside group behavior
- panic that becomes pattern

## Campaign Use

School Of Tiny Fish should represent rumor networks, group instincts, and
ordinary locals who know more together than any one person can say.

They may appear as students, shopkeepers, office workers, surfers, or commuters
whose tiny reactions reveal where pressure is moving.

Use School Of Tiny Fish when the campaign needs:

- a crowd that behaves like an omen
- clues distributed across many minor witnesses
- a soft alternative to centralized authority

## Table Motifs

- heads turning at the same time
- messages relayed without anyone claiming authorship
- crowds parting around an unseen danger
- identical small gestures across unrelated NPCs
- a person who says "everyone knows" but cannot name who told them
