# Ranger Level 1 Spells

Class guide: [[Player Handouts/Class Guides/Ranger|Ranger]]

Use this as a spell menu for a level 1 ranger. It lists cantrip availability and level 1 spells from the 2024 D&D Basic Rules.

Special tags:

- C = Concentration
- R = Ritual
- M = specific Material component

At level 1, a ranger prepares two level 1 ranger spells and has two level 1 spell slots. Hunter's Mark is always prepared and can be cast without spending a spell slot a limited number of times per Long Rest.

Rangers do not normally know cantrips at level 1. At level 2, a ranger who chooses the Druidic Warrior Fighting Style learns two druid cantrips; use [[Player Handouts/Spell Lists/Druid Level 1 Spells|Druid Level 1 Spells]] for those options.
