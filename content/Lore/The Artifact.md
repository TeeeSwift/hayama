# The Artifact

The Artifact is a failed creation of [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]],
resulting from an attempt to separate divine essence from an Anchored being.

## Origin

The Anchored sought to externalize their divinity into a physical vessel so
that they could relinquish their divine nature and live as mortals.

The experiment failed, producing an unstable object containing concentrated
divine energy.

## Containment

Unable to destroy the Artifact, the Anchored buried it within a remote
mountain outside Hayama.

While the Barrier remained intact, its dampening effects kept the Artifact
dormant.

## Current Effects

With the Barrier now weakened, the Artifact has begun to leak divine energy
into the surrounding environment.

This has resulted in:

- The emergence of fae-like entities in the mountains
- Localized distortions of reality
- The appearance of a third, misleading threat unrelated to Watatsumi's
  reclaiming

These entities are not aligned with either Watatsumi or the Anchored, but are
instead a byproduct of uncontrolled divinity.
