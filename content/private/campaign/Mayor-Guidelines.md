# The Mayor — GM Guidelines

## Core Principle

The Mayor is not a source of truth.

He is:

> a system operator working with incomplete information under pressure

Escalation should increase:
- attention
- urgency

But NOT:
- clarity
- completeness

---

## Ryuta Connection Rules

- Communication is **event-driven**, not player-controlled
- The Mayor cannot be summoned or called reliably
- Information is:
  - fragmented
  - context-dependent
  - often misinterpreted

Ryuta functions as:

> a diagnostic signal leaking from a stressed system

---

## In-Person Interaction Rules

### 1. Answers the Wrong Layer

Players ask about people → Mayor answers about systems

### 2. Context, Not Explanation

He describes patterns, not causes

### 3. Partial Presence

- distracted
- interrupted
- multitasking cognitively

### 4. Confident but Incorrect

- operates on outdated assumptions
- may be wrong in critical ways

### 5. Blind Spots

The Mayor initially does NOT know:
- Zenji’s revival
- Marin’s condition
- Teru’s investigation

---

## Dialogue Patterns

### Adjacent Answers

Question: "What is happening?"
Answer: "There have been irregularities in coastal behavior."

---

### System Framing

Question: "Who caused this?"
Answer: "Cause is rarely singular."

---

### Data Extraction

Mayor asks instead of answering:

"At what depth?"
"How long did it persist?"

---

## Mayor Voice Fragments

Use short, incomplete statements:

- "That should not be there."
- "You are too close to it."
- "This is not contained."
- "That pattern is incorrect."
- "This should not be surfacing here."

---

## Safe Answer Patterns

Use these when players push directly:

- "You are asking the wrong question."
- "That is a symptom, not a cause."
- "I do not have visibility on that."
- "That does not correspond to any known pattern."
- "It is not where it should be."
- "This is outside expected parameters."

---

## Escalation Template

### Phase 1 — Noise
- fragmented statements
- no recognition of Ryuta

### Phase 2 — Recognition
- "You again"
- reactive observations

### Phase 3 — Utilization
- asks Ryuta questions
- gives minimal directives

### Phase 4 — Strain
- urgency increases
- contradictions appear
- system clearly failing

---

## Scene Template: Mayor Interaction

**Setup:** Players encounter the Mayor during civic activity

**Flow:**

1. Players ask direct question
2. Mayor reframes into system language
3. Mayor asks 1–2 questions back
4. Provides partial statement
5. Is interrupted or disengages

**Example:**

Player: "What is happening to Marin?"

Mayor:
"There have been irregularities in coastal expression."

Pause.

"Where did you observe this?"

Player answers.

Mayor:
"That is not consistent with expected behavior."

(Interrupted by aide / leaves)

---

## Final Rule

After any interaction, players should feel:

> "We learned something… but we don’t understand it yet"

Never:

> "We now know what is going on"
