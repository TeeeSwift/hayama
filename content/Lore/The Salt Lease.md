## Summary

The founders of [[Hayama]] were not humans, but Spirits of the Foam and Shore
(perhaps Kodama of the coastal trees or Iso-onago). They desired to live as
mortals—to experience the brevity, heat, and passion of human life. The great
sea god [[Watatsumi]] granted this, but like the tide, all things he gives must
eventually "ebb."

## The Clauses

### I. The Gift of Flesh (肉身の賦与)

Watatsumi distilled the brine and the "Cotton-Foam" of the waves to weave bodies
for the spirits.

The spirits became human. They gained the ability to walk on soil, to feel the sun, and to reproduce.

Their blood remains salty. When a resident of Hayama dies, they
must be "returned" to the sea (sky burials or ground burials are forbidden;
only sea burials are permitted).

### II. The Gift of the Receding Shelf (干潟の供与)

1,000 years ago, the area of Hayama was underwater or a jagged, uninhabitable
reef.

Watatsumi commanded the Kuroshio (Black Tide) to pull back
permanently, revealing the white sands and the habitable hills. He tamed the
typhoons so the town would never be leveled.

He gifted the town the "Three Silver Treasures" (The Whale,
the Sea Bream, and the Kelp) ensuring they would never starve as long as the
Lease held.

### III. The Term of the Lease (千年の期限)

This is the "Salt" in the lease. He did not give the land; he loaned it.

For 1,000 years, the salt would stay in the rocks and the
water would stay in the basin.

Upon the rising of the 13,000th Moon (the end of the 1,000th
lunar year), the "Lease on the Salt" expires. The internal salt that holds
the citizens' human forms together will begin to dissolve, and the sea will
reclaim the shelf.


### IV. The Hidden Clause

As per the [[Providence and Heavenly Mandate#The Law of Agency|Law of Agency]],
[[Watatsumi]], must provide a "Way Out". A contract that offers no escape isn't
a "Lease" — it's an execution. 

Watatsumi provides the following paths for his subjects to renew the lease.

#### The Path of Sacrifice (犠牲)

A descendant of the original founders must willingly surrender their "humanity"
to become the eternal guardian of the tide, effectively renewing the contract
with their own spirit.

> [!quote] 
> Life for Life

#### The Path of Mending (繕い)

The city must find and return the Blue Pearl (青真珠)—the crystallized essence
of a Blue Moon—which Watatsumi "lost" when he created the spirits' bodies.

> [!quote]
> The Debt Repaid

#### The Path of Transcendence (昇華)

The people of Hayama must prove they are no longer just "Spirits in Salt-Flesh"
but a new race entirely. This involves a trial of cultural or spiritual merit at
the underwater Deep Shrine.

> [!quote]
> Evolved Form
