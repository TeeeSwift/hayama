Members of [[The Morning Tide]] who lost faith with Watatsumi over the 1000
year lease


**Origin:** They are lesser gods of the coast who became mortal.

**Key Past Event:** They decided to reproduce, which ends their immortality. And as the
 generations continued, they lost touch with [[Watatsumi]].

**Current Goal:** Just living normally with no goals. While they are no longer
 immortal, their divine spark has been passed down the generations. It
 manifests in individuals who have inexplicable luck or abilities associated
 with the ocean. A lucky fisherman, a gifted diver, a navigator who can see
 stars just a little clearer.
