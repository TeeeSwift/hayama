# Coral

## Role

Coral is an Anchored concept for collective architecture, slow construction, and
living systems that become stone-like structure.

Use this concept for an Anchored whose power is communal, accretive, and
dangerously dependent on environmental conditions.

## Natural Model

Corals are colonies of tiny animals that build hard calcium carbonate
structures. Over long periods, those structures become reefs: shelter, barrier,
habitat, archive, and hazard.

Coral reefs are vibrant but fragile. Heat, pollution, acidification, and
disrupted relationships can cause bleaching and collapse.

This makes Coral a figure of:

- many lives making one structure
- protection built slowly
- beauty as infrastructure
- dependence on mutual conditions
- fragility hidden inside hardness

## Campaign Use

Coral should represent collective survival that can become brittle.

They may be tied to old family networks, neighborhood obligations, hidden
committees, or ritual systems built by many small contributions over centuries.

Use Coral when the campaign needs to ask:

- when community becomes a wall
- what happens when a protective system bleaches
- whether a structure made by many can be blamed on one person

## Table Motifs

- branching diagrams of favors and obligations
- rooms full of small contributions from many households
- damage that spreads because one relationship fails
- pale versions of things that should be colorful
- a person who says "we" before they say "I"
