# Druid Level 1 Spells

Class guide: [[Player Handouts/Class Guides/Druid|Druid]]

Use this as a spell menu for a level 1 druid. It lists cantrips and level 1 spells from the 2024 D&D Basic Rules.

Special tags:

- C = Concentration
- R = Ritual
- M = specific Material component

At level 1, a druid knows two cantrips and prepares four level 1 druid spells. Druids regain spell slots after a Long Rest and can change prepared level 1+ spells after a Long Rest. In 2024 rules, Speak with Animals is always prepared from the Druidic feature.

Damage and healing notes use your druid spellcasting ability, Wisdom. If a spell says "save," the target rolls against your Spell Save DC. If a spell says "spell attack," you roll with your Spell Attack Bonus.

## Druid Cantrips

| Spell | School | Special | At The Table |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Druidcraft | Transmutation |  | Small nature omen or nature trick. No damage; predicts nearby weather, opens flowers, creates tiny sensory effects, or lights/snuffs small flames. |
| Elementalism | Transmutation |  | Small elemental manipulation. No damage; shape or move a small amount of air, earth, fire, or water for utility and flavor. |
| Guidance | Divination | C | Touch or guide a creature before an ability check. It can add `1d4` to the check while the spell lasts. |
| Mending | Transmutation |  | Repairs a small break or tear in an object. No damage; takes time and cannot restore magic by itself. |
| Message | Transmutation |  | Sends a whispered message to a creature and lets it whisper back. No damage; useful for stealth and coordination. |
| Poison Spray | Necromancy |  | Target makes a Constitution save. Failed save takes `1d12` poison damage. Many creatures resist or ignore poison, so check the target. |
| Produce Flame | Conjuration |  | Creates flame in your hand for light. You can make a ranged spell attack with it; on a hit, it deals `1d8` fire damage. |
| Resistance | Abjuration | C | Helps a creature resist danger. The target can usually add `1d4` to one saving throw while the spell lasts. |
| Shillelagh | Transmutation |  | Empowers a club or quarterstaff. For the duration, you can use Wisdom for attacks with it and its damage die becomes magical, usually `1d8`. |
| Spare the Dying | Necromancy |  | Stabilizes a dying creature at 0 HP. No healing and no damage; stops death saves from continuing. |
| Starry Wisp | Evocation |  | Ranged spell attack. On a hit, deals `1d8` radiant damage and briefly makes the target glow, helping reveal it. |
| Thorn Whip | Transmutation |  | Melee spell attack at range. On a hit, deals `1d6` piercing damage and can pull the target closer. |
| Thunderclap | Evocation |  | Creatures near you make a Constitution save. Failed save takes `1d6` thunder damage. Loud and risky around bystanders. |

## Level 1 Druid Spells

| Spell | School | Special | At The Table |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animal Friendship | Enchantment |  | A Beast makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, it is Charmed by you for the duration or until harmed. No damage. |
| Charm Person | Enchantment |  | One Humanoid makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, it is Charmed by you. No damage; social consequences may follow afterward. |
| Create or Destroy Water | Transmutation |  | Creates clean water or destroys open water in a container or area. No damage by default; strong utility for survival, fire, clues, and water problems. |
| Cure Wounds | Abjuration |  | Touch a creature to heal it. Level 1 healing is usually `2d8 + your Wisdom modifier`. Does not affect Constructs or Undead unless your rules say otherwise. |
| Detect Magic | Divination | C, R | Sense active magic nearby and read its school with focus. No damage; blocked by enough material or distance. |
| Detect Poison and Disease | Divination | C, R | Sense poisons, poisonous creatures, and diseases nearby. No damage; useful for food, water, bodies, plants, and suspicious rooms. |
| Entangle | Conjuration | C | Plants or grasping growth fill an area. Creatures make Strength saves; failed targets are Restrained until they escape. No damage by default. |
| Faerie Fire | Evocation | C | Creatures and objects in an area make Dexterity saves. Failed targets glow; attacks against them have advantage and invisibility stops helping them. No damage. |
| Fog Cloud | Conjuration | C | Creates a heavily obscuring fog area. No damage; useful for escape, cover, confusion, and blocking sight-based effects. |
| Goodberry | Conjuration |  | Creates magical berries. Each berry restores `1 HP` and provides nourishment for a day. Excellent out-of-combat healing. |
| Healing Word | Abjuration |  | Bonus Action ranged healing. Level 1 healing is usually `2d4 + your Wisdom modifier`. Good for picking up a downed ally. |
| Ice Knife | Conjuration |  | Ranged spell attack. Hit deals `1d10` piercing damage, then the shard bursts; nearby creatures make Dexterity saves or take `2d6` cold damage. |
| Jump | Transmutation |  | Touch a creature to increase its jump distance for the duration. No damage; useful for gaps, ledges, rooftops, and escapes. |
| Longstrider | Transmutation |  | Increases a creature's Speed, usually by 10 feet, for the duration. No damage and no Concentration. |
| Protection from Evil and Good | Abjuration | C, M | Protects a creature from certain supernatural creature types. Those creatures have trouble attacking, charming, frightening, or possessing the target. No damage. |
| Purify Food and Drink | Transmutation | R | Makes nonmagical food and drink safe from poison and disease. No damage. |
| Speak with Animals | Divination | R | Lets you communicate with Beasts for the duration. No damage; animals answer from their own perspective. |
| Thunderwave | Evocation |  | Creatures in a close cube make Constitution saves. Failed save takes `2d8` thunder damage and is pushed away; successful save takes half and is not pushed. |
