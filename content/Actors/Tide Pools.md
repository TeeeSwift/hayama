# Tide Pools

## Role

Tide Pools is an Anchored concept for small contained worlds, temporary refuge,
and ecosystems left behind when the sea withdraws.

Use this concept for an Anchored who protects pockets of life at the cost of
isolation.

## Natural Model

Tide pools form in rock depressions that hold seawater after the tide goes out.
They can contain algae, snails, crabs, small fish, anemones, and other life
until the sea returns.

They are rich, beautiful, exposed, and precarious. Temperature, salinity, oxygen,
sunlight, and predators can change quickly.

This makes Tide Pools a figure of:

- temporary sanctuary
- isolation after withdrawal
- small worlds with harsh limits
- survival between larger cycles
- protection that can become exposure

## Campaign Use

Tide Pools should represent contained pockets of memory, divinity, or community.

They may guard small safe rooms, hidden neighborhoods, emergency refuges, or
sealed memory spaces inside the Barrier system.

Use Tide Pools when the campaign needs:

- a safe place that cannot remain safe forever
- a contained scene where the outside crisis is temporarily suspended
- a question about whether protection should reconnect or stay sealed

## Table Motifs

- bowls, basins, courtyards, sheltered rooms
- small trapped reflections of larger events
- people surviving in a pocket of calm
- rapid changes when conditions shift
- someone checking levels, temperature, and air
