A Buddhist abbot who accidentally reanimated [[Actors/Zenji|Zenji]] during a
funerary release rite, and has since been drawn into the hidden divine systems
of Hayama.

## Role

Teru serves as the abbot of a local temple.

He performs:
- funerary rites
- memorial services
- spiritual guidance for the community

His practice is:
- disciplined
- compassionate
- grounded in tradition

## The Incident

At a neglected memorial site, Teru performed a rite intended to:

> calm or release a lingering spirit

Due to instability in [[Lore/The Barrier|The Barrier]] system, the ritual did
not behave as expected.

Instead of releasing a spirit, it created a pathway through which
[[Actors/Zenji|Zenji]] returned.

The ritual has not behaved consistently since.

## Current Situation

Teru is working with Zenji to understand what has happened.

He:
- revisits ritual sites
- repeats rites to observe differences
- investigates locations that feel "incorrect"
- speaks with locals about subtle disturbances

He has become a ground-level observer of system instability.

## Relationship with Zenji

Their relationship is:
- quiet
- uncertain
- iterative

Zenji provides incomplete guidance based on recognition of patterns.

Teru executes those ideas in the physical world.

Neither fully understands what they are doing.

## Thematic Role

Teru represents:

- correct intention within an incomplete system
- human spiritual frameworks encountering deeper structures
- responsibility without understanding

He has been drawn into a layer of Hayama that he did not know existed.
