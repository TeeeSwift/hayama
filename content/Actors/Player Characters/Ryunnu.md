# Ryunnu

## Elevator Pitch

A newcomer to Hayama who has been mistakenly registered as a valid interface
for the Mayor, creating an unintended and poorly understood connection to an
Anchored deity.

---

## Surface Identity

- Recently arrived in Hayama
- Electrical management systems salesman
- D&D background: Noble
- Class: Warlock
- Curious, somewhat uncertain, trying new experiences
- Enrolls in a local diving class shortly after arriving

Ryunnu approaches the town as an outsider, without the inherited context that
shapes many long-term residents.

Despite not actually being an electrician, multiple people in Hayama continue to
mistakenly treat him like one.

This becomes a recurring social pattern.

People casually ask him to:

- look at breaker panels
- inspect strange flickering lights
- diagnose appliance issues
- explain wiring problems
- check grounding faults
- help with electrical maintenance

Ryunnu repeatedly has to explain:

> I sell systems. I do not install them.

The town rarely seems to care about the distinction.

## The Error

A divine system responsible for managing relationships between Anchored and
other entities has misidentified Ryunnu as a valid interface for
[[Actors/The Mayor|The Mayor]].

This was not:

- requested
- intended
- noticed immediately

The result is a shared channel between them.

---

## Nature of the Connection

The connection is:

- intermittent
- bidirectional
- poorly understood by both parties

Ryunnu occasionally:

- hears fragments of the Mayor’s thoughts
- feels a presence or pressure that does not belong to him

The Mayor may:

- briefly perceive through Ryunnu
- receive stray impressions

These interactions are:

- rare
- incomplete
- lacking context

Ryunnu does not receive guidance, answers, or clear direction.

---

## Relationship with the Mayor

The tone of the connection is:

- polite
- awkward
- slightly embarrassed

Neither party chose this connection.

The Mayor is aware of it, but treats it as a minor anomaly.

Ryunnu is left to interpret experiences that feel significant but provide no
clear meaning.

## Local Relationships

- Brazilian Jiu Jitsu instructor: an older, practical teacher who helps Ryunnu
  with body control, distance, and staying calm under pressure
- Old man who wants electrical help: still thinks Ryunnu is an electrician no
  matter how many times Ryunnu explains that he works in sales
- Local event organizer: runs speed dating events; somehow got Ryunnu's contact
  information and sent him a list of matches after he was accidentally included
  in several rounds

Ryunnu sometimes goes to cafes to work on his computer. A few times, he has been
mistakenly folded into speed dating rounds. Women sit down at his table, he
tries to clarify, and sometimes he simply gives in and participates.

## First Incident: Nagisabashi Cafe

During early strange electrical issues at
[[Locations/Nagisabashi Cafe|Nagisabashi Cafe]], Ryunnu is asked to look at
lighting instability, refrigeration resets, and inconsistent system behavior.

The problems initially appear mundane:

- breaker irregularities
- timing desynchronization
- refrigeration faults
- strange power draw
- network instability
- inconsistent timestamps

During the first major reaping event, Ryunnu notices something impossible:

the cafe's systems continue recognizing someone after people begin failing to do
so.

Possible examples:

- a POS terminal keeps an open tab active
- a digital system refuses to remove a name
- timestamps continue tracking someone people stop noticing
- electrical load remains associated with an apparently empty table
- security systems continue acknowledging a person after social recognition
  begins failing

Ryunnu becomes one of the first people to encounter hard evidence that the town's
systems are becoming internally inconsistent.

---

## Relationship To Infrastructure

Ryunnu is not a deep infrastructure specialist. He sells electrical management
systems, which means he knows enough to talk about equipment and customers, but
he is not the person who installs or repairs anything.

Where others perceive:

- emotional wrongness
- spiritual pressure
- social instability
- memory distortion

Ryunnu notices:

- when people misunderstand what he actually does
- when a social situation becomes hard to escape
- when a customer, organizer, or local contact is trying to pull him into a role
  he did not ask for
- electrical behavior that is strange enough that even a salesman knows it is
  not normal

This makes Ryunnu unusually sensitive to the interaction between reclaiming,
records, infrastructure, and hidden systems.

---

## Thematic Role

Ryunnu represents:

- systems misidentifying individuals
- assignment without consent
- connection without understanding
- infrastructure remembering differently than people do
- being treated as part of a system that does not recognize you correctly

---

## Campaign Role

- Early: confusion and fragmented perception
- Mid: recognition that the connection is not intentional or stable
- Late: potential interaction with the system that created the error

His presence highlights that even functioning systems can produce
misaligned outcomes.
