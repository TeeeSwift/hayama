Watatsumi, 綿津見 is one the major gods of the sea.

It's name combines

- Wata: Old Japanese for ocean (likely derived from a crossing, "wataru")
- Tsu: just a combining possessive particle like "no", also means port/crossing
- Mi: Watcher

## Mythology

Most years, it cycles through its 12 forms coinciding with the moon, with a 13th
form on the occasional blue moon (every 2-3 years)

**13. The Blue Moon Form: 冥海月 (Meikaigetsu)**

The Moon-Drunk Jellyfish / The Dark Sea Moon

The 13th moon is an "extra" moon—an anomaly in the calendar. In Japanese
folklore, things that fall outside the natural order are often seen as Yō
kai-adjacent or hauntingly beautiful.

The god sheds its physical weight entirely and becomes a colossal, ethereal
Kurage (Jellyfish) that floats above the water rather than in it. Its bells are
translucent violet, and its tentacles are miles-long strands of starlight that
reach down into the deep.

This form is known as the "Mirror of the Void." During a Blue Moon, the sea god
is said to be "drunk" on celestial light. He forgets his duty to the tides and
becomes a dreamer.

Game Mechanics:

- Gravity Shift: The sea becomes "weightless" near him. Players might find
  themselves swimming through the air.
- Memory Leak: The god emits a psychic hum. Those nearby must save or lose a
  memory, which manifests as a glowing bubble in the water.
- The Blue Gate: This form acts as a literal portal to the Lunar Plane or the
  Far Realm.
