There are certain laws that providence（摂理) and heavenly mandates must adhere to.


## The Law of Agency

Also known as the Doctrine of the Open Path (開道の教理), this mandates that "No
destiny shall be a cage without a key."

> [!quote]
> 人の意志ある限り、運命は定まらず。
> As long as there is human will, destiny is not fixed.

- The Doctrine: No deity may impose a "Closed Loop."
- The Rule: For every divine curse, there must be a cure; for every debt, a
  means of repayment; and for every destiny, a "Crack of Will" (意志の隙間)
  through which a mortal might escape.
