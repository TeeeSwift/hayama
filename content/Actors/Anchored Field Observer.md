# Anchored Field Observer

## Role

An Anchored operative who appears at anomaly sites, checks old markers, removes
traces, and monitors the failing Barrier without explaining what they are doing.

This character should be named when they become active at the table. Until then,
use this note as the functional slot for the first visible field presence of
[[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]].

## Location

They can appear anywhere the Barrier is showing symptoms:

- shoreline stabilization site
- drainage channels
- old stone markers
- shrine edges
- service alleys
- storm drains
- seawalls
- abandoned or locked coastal structures
- places where the sea leaves impossible objects behind

They should rarely appear in places of ordinary social comfort. They are more
likely to be seen at thresholds, damage points, and liminal infrastructure.

## Faction

- [[Actors/The Anchored|The Anchored]]

They are not necessarily a leader. They are a field-level observer, containment
agent, surveyor, or ritual technician.

Publicly, they may pass as:

- a surveyor
- utility inspector
- shrine maintenance worker
- environmental monitor
- old-family assistant
- quiet passerby with the wrong tools for the scene

Privately, they are monitoring the Barrier's failures and deciding what evidence
must be preserved, sealed, or removed.

## Narrative Function

They provide suspicion.

Use this character when the story needs the players to ask:

- why someone arrived before the authorities
- why evidence vanished
- who knows more than they are saying
- whether the Anchored caused the incident
- why old infrastructure matters
- whether containment and cover-up are different things

This character should often be doing the right thing in the least trustworthy
possible way.

## What They Represent

They represent control as protection and secrecy as harm.

Their implied philosophy:

> Panic wastes time. Explanation wastes more.

They embody:

- containment
- inherited duty
- institutional secrecy
- competence without trust
- the moral cost of managing a crisis from the shadows

They are the first human-scale face of the Anchored's core contradiction: they
may be trying to save the town, but they have made themselves impossible to
trust.

## What They Provide The Story

### Suspicious Evidence Handling

They can:

- remove a barnacled stone fragment before officials notice it
- mark old infrastructure with chalk, thread, salt, or ink
- seal a minor tide leak without explaining how
- warn the party not to touch an object
- take notes in a script or shorthand tied to the Barrier
- appear at multiple incidents before the party connects them

### Containment Clues

They can reveal through action, not exposition, that:

- the incidents are connected
- the shoreline breach is not isolated
- sacred sites and civic infrastructure overlap
- someone has been monitoring the system for a long time
- the Anchored fear escalation more than blame

### Early Antagonism Without Villainy

They may obstruct the party by:

- withholding explanations
- taking evidence
- refusing to name the danger
- warning witnesses into silence
- blocking access to unstable sites

But they should not feel cruel. They should feel controlled, tired, and deeply
accustomed to not being believed.

## Partial Powers Or Methods

Their work can feel halfway between ritual and field engineering.

Possible methods:

- salt-thread grids
- old brass measuring tools
- folded paper seals
- tide charts annotated with impossible times
- listening rods pressed against stone
- shell dust used like forensic powder
- knots tied around damaged markers
- small offerings placed like emergency patches

They may have limited power to:

- stabilize a minor manifestation briefly
- identify a stressed Barrier point
- tell whether something has been touched by Watatsumi's reclaiming
- prevent one witness from being forgotten for a short period
- close a threshold at the cost of making another one worse

## First Scene

After the first reaping event, the party notices someone quietly scraping
barnacles from fresh concrete and sealing the residue in a small glass vial.

If confronted, they say:

> If you saw this, then you are already too close.

Then they try to leave with the evidence.

## Voice

Precise, restrained, impatient with obvious questions.

They speak like someone measuring how much truth the room can survive.
