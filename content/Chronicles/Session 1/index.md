# Session 1

Related GM notes: [[Chronicles/Campaign Prep/Debriefs/Session 1 Debrief|Session
1 Debrief]]

## Quick Summary

Session 1 opened with ordinary [[Atlas/Hayama|Hayama]] doing its best impression
of a cozy seaside town: morning yoga, reheated curry, a breakfast banana, cafe
errands, and the ironclad local belief that
[[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]] must secretly be an electrician.

Then [[Actors/Watatsumi|the sea]] started misbehaving.

[[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Marin]] felt the boundary between
herself and the water soften during a beginner dive. A violent current scattered
the class and nearly took [[Sato-san]]. Later, at
[[Atlas/Nagisabashi Coffee]], a deep-sea rift opened in public, drowned an old
woman in front of the people capable of perceiving it, and erased her from
everyone else's memory.

By the end of the day, the party had two impossible facts in hand: Ryunnu's
regulator should not have worked, and the cafe had one extra phone where a woman
used to be.

## Morning Cuts

### Marin: Yoga, Gear Checks, And A Bad Smell On The Wind

[[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Marin]] woke up for her morning beach
yoga and immediately ran into [[Actors/Marin's Father|her father]], who was
finishing the news and preparing to vanish into the sacred parental fog known as
"important business."

He thanked her for covering the beginner diving class. The day's students were
[[Sato-san]], an elderly regular, and a newcomer named
[[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]]. He told her to double-check
everything, especially the regulators, since the shop only had two working ones
left. Then he eyed the ocean, sniffed the air like an extremely suspicious
weather station, and told her to avoid the [[Shinnase]] area.

The official reason: the vibes by that seawall were funky.

The unofficial reason: the seawall was gross.

Marin answered with the curt efficiency of a rebellious high school student who
has heard all of this before. Her father left for [[Inamura]] to handle
"equipment procurement," then immediately reappeared loading a surfboard onto
his moped. He cheerfully explained that he might have to check the waters while
he was over there. You never know.

Marin went to the beach. Her calendar had two sacred appointments: beginner
lesson, then parfaits.

### Ryunnu: Nightmare, Curry, Coffee, And Professional Misclassification

In a modern apartment in [[Horiuchi]],
[[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]] woke from a nightmare.

He had been floating as if underwater, wrapped and squeezed by something with no
clear shape. He wanted to breathe. He wanted to get free. Neither request made
it very far up the chain of command.

He woke panting, sweating, and desperately thirsty. He went to the water cooler,
not the sink, and carefully mixed hot and cold water until it reached the exact
lukewarm midpoint his soul required. Something seemed to move at the edge of his
vision, but when he looked properly, there was nothing to catch.

Then came coffee. Ryunnu is not merely a man who drinks coffee. Ryunnu wakes up
early enough to have a relationship with coffee.

Breakfast was leftover Japanese chicken curry, reheated in a pot, with rice in
the microwave. He thought he heard something suspicious, but the microwave was
too loud to let the supernatural have a clean entrance. After eating, he checked
his schedule:

- take a beginner diving lesson
- fix some electrical equipment at a nearby [[Atlas/Nagisabashi Coffee|cafe]]

Ryunnu is in electrical project management. He has said this many times.
[[Atlas/Hayama|Hayama]], collectively, has decided this is close enough.

### Alex: Seismology, Breakfast, And A Pearl Waiting For Its Moment

In another apartment, [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex Gaga]] chatted
with a friend from her computer. The apartment was older than Ryunnu's, but not
old-old. Lived-in. A little softer at the edges. Alex, with her red pixie cut,
was asked whether she would attend the next mantle seismology consortium.

She said yes, probably, but maybe for the last time. Science and research were
starting to lose their hold on her.

Then breakfast smells drifted up from downstairs and made a stronger argument
than academia.

At the restaurant below, Alex found [[Actors/Mollusk|Beatrice]], her landlord
and the owner, making breakfast. They talked about Alex's musical performance
the night before. It had not been packed, exactly, but [[Kondo-san]] in the
kitchen tapped his foot all night, which counts for something. Beatrice told
Alex she could perform and promote events there whenever she wanted. There were
even old flyers left over, patiently waiting for ambition to return.

Then Beatrice gave Alex a beautiful golden bracelet set with a single pearl.
She said she had been waiting for the tides to tell her when the moment was
right. Things, including people, shine brightest when they find the person they
can come home to.

She also reminded Alex that an artist's work is to take the pain of the world
and turn it into something beautiful.

Alex thanked her and went back upstairs to do makeup before her afternoon
meeting with [[Actors/Player Characters/Vincent Uminashi|Vincent]].

### Vincent: The Banana Arrives Exactly On Schedule

In a large bedroom inside a much larger house by the shore,
[[Actors/Player Characters/Vincent Uminashi|Vincent]] woke up. He is 27. His
life has systems.

At the perfect moment, [[Actors/Chizuru|Chizuru]] knocked and delivered his
daily breakfast banana. Vincent thanked her curtly, barely looking up.

Chizuru explained that she needed to go to town hall to file paperwork for
Vincent's father's company, so she would not be able to make him lunch. She had
therefore adjusted his afternoon appointment into a brunch meeting at a cafe.
Vincent replied with the full emotional range of "ok."

His sailboat repairs were finished, and the boat was waiting at the dock on the
house's private beach. Vincent did not want to test it himself, but he did,
because wealth still occasionally demands chores.

Out on [[Actors/Watatsumi|the water]], he pushed the boat through sharp turns
and impressive speed without much effort. Ranger behavior, frankly. When he
stopped to approve the repairs, a massive school of tiny silver fish circled the
boat, forming a tall spiral in the water. From above, it looked like a mirror or
a moon.

Then the fish scattered all at once.

Nothing visible had frightened them.

Vincent registered this with a profound internal "huh, weird," returned to
shore, and went back to his room to read philosophy in Greek until his meeting.

## The Diving Lesson

The beginner lesson started normally enough.
[[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]] was surprisingly comfortable in the
water and deeply impressed by swimming fins, which honestly deserved their
moment. [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|Marin]] watched him and
[[Sato-san]] explore from a safe distance.

Then Marin felt the water change.

The membrane between her and the sea thinned. There was no clear edge anymore:
no sinking, no buoyancy, no effort required to stay where she was. Even her
emotions flattened into perfect ease. She did not need to surface. She did not
need to protect her students. She did not even need to breathe.

For one impossible moment, everything was harmony.

Then a current hit like a sideways gust of wind.

The class scattered. Sato-san's regulator was knocked loose. Panic bloomed fast
underwater, but Marin and the others managed to regroup and stabilize him before
the lesson became a tragedy.

Back on shore, [[Sato-san]] and Ryunnu assured Marin that they understood
[[Actors/Watatsumi|the ocean]] had moods, and that they did not blame the
diving school. They even promised to come back. This may be bravery. It may be
denial. It may be the specific tourist logic that says a near-death experience
is fine if nobody writes a bad review.

On the way out, they passed [[Actors/Marin's Father|Marin's father]], returning
from his important business and possibly also surfing. Entirely unaware of the
terror that had just occurred, he pitched them a membership, a punch card, and
the cafe the diving shop was apparently opening.

Marin endured this with the quiet shame of a teenager whose parent has become
marketing collateral.

Then, while breaking down the gear, she found Ryunnu's regulator tube punctured
and full of water.

Water had been pumping through his regulator the entire time.

## The Cafe Meeting

At [[Atlas/Nagisabashi Coffee|Nagisabashi Coffee]], Alex and Vincent sat across
from [[Actors/Hayashi|Hayashi-san]], a clean-cut municipal salaryman in a suit,
while [[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]] worked nearby on the cafe's
electrical circuitry. He was polite, tidy, slightly timid, and extremely relieved
to be giving someone else a problem.

There had been complaints about the [[Atlas/Uminashi Seawall|Uminashi seawall]],
completed two months ago. Most were ordinary civic grumbling. One was not. A
complaint had pointed to a possible structural deficiency, and because public safety was involved,
[[Atlas/Hayama|the town]] asked the coast guard to perform a simple scan during
patrol.

The scan confirmed that the foundation was exposed on the third span of the
north wall.

[[Actors/Hayashi|Hayashi]] explained that a third-party structural audit was now
required. Vincent texted [[Actors/Chizuru|Chizuru]] to ask who had been
responsible for the construction project. [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa
Marin|Marin]] arrived for parfaits and settled into the corner as
[[Actors/Hayashi|Hayashi]], visibly lighter after transferring the problem into
richer hands, excused himself.

## The First Reaping

After [[Actors/Hayashi|Hayashi]] left, a strange quiet settled over the
[[Atlas/Nagisabashi Coffee|cafe]].

Sound became muffled, as though the whole room had been pushed far below the
surface. The air gained weight. Pressure gathered in the corners. A rift opened,
showing the cold blue-green dark of the deep sea beyond it.

A dense, slime-like current began pouring through.

Alex and Vincent could barely move. The pressure felt absurdly vast, like the
weight of [[Actors/Watatsumi|an ocean]] trying to remember where it belonged.
Most of the cafe seemed stopped in time, unaware of the room changing around
them.

A thin layer of water spread across the floor.

Vincent realized that all four PCs were caught inside the event and still
conscious, along with the old woman:

- [[Actors/Player Characters/Alex Gaga|Alex]]
- [[Actors/Player Characters/Vincent Uminashi|Vincent]]
- [[Actors/Player Characters/Ryunnu|Ryunnu]], still near the cafe circuitry
- [[Actors/Player Characters/Aizawa Marin|the high schooler]] eating parfait in the corner
- an old woman sitting with two friends

The water found the old woman.

At first it climbed slowly over her feet, but water only looks patient until it
decides not to be. It crawled up her legs, soaked into her clothes, and pulled
itself over her torso. She screamed, but the sound went nowhere. The cafe held
only a muffled hum.

When the water reached her face, it entered her mouth like air. She had no time
to swallow. No time to fight properly. She drowned from the inside while the
world pretended not to hear.

A small crab crawled from her mouth and toward her eye.

Her body went limp. The water tugged her through the rift. Then the rift closed.

Time resumed.

Nobody seemed to have seen anything.

The woman's two friends asked a passing waiter why there was a third cup of
coffee at their table. The waiter apologized and took it away.

Vincent saw a cell phone left on the seat where the old woman had been.
