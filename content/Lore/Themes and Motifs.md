# Themes and Motifs

## Core Themes

- **Misdiagnosis vs Understanding**  
  The true nature of the system is consistently misinterpreted before it is understood.

- **Control vs Transformation**  
  Attempts to control or extract divinity lead to instability, while transformation produces more stable outcomes.

- **Continuity vs Change**  
  Some forces persist across generations, while others emerge and reshape the system.

- **Natural Cycle vs Artificial Stability**  
  The Barrier represents an imposed stability against an inevitable natural process.

- **Responsibility vs Unintended Consequence**  
  Many key actors contribute to the crisis without fully understanding their role in it.

## Motifs

- **The Sea as a System**  
  The ocean behaves less like a willful force and more like a responsive, pressure-seeking system.

- **Pressure and Flow**  
  Divinity accumulates, disperses, redirects, and seeks equilibrium.

- **Transformation of the Unwanted**  
  Pain, instability, or excess becomes something new rather than being removed.

- **Convergence in Darkness**  
  Things that are meant to meet will find each other across distance, time, or obscurity.

- **The Unseen Made Visible**  
  Different characters perceive different layers of reality depending on their relationship to divinity.
